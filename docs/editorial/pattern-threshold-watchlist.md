# Dead Air — Pattern-Threshold Watchlist

> Operational mirror. The authoritative project records remain in the Dead Air Google Drive and the Emergent Canon Protocol governs mythology and continuity.

## Purpose

Maintain a small, actively pruned list of details worth noticing when reading new transcripts because they resemble unresolved dimensions from prior cases. The Watchlist is **not future canon**, a confidence ladder, or evidence of a shared cause. Its purpose is to improve attention while preserving the independence of each case.

## Canon firewall

- A Watchlist entry may identify a dimension worth checking, but it does not authorize a connection, recurring law, entity, mechanism, faction, hidden truth, or adaptation choice.
- A resemblance recorded during intake remains source-derived or observational unless a later approved process changes its status.
- Do not steer a case toward the Watchlist. The source dossier, central interpretation, incident hierarchy, and adaptation plan must arise from the case itself.
- Negative evidence, failed resemblance, ordinary explanations, and material differences count as useful results.
- Do not count a resemblance as a completed cross-case recurrence while the case is unfinished.

## DA-003 grandfathering boundary

DA-003 predates Pattern-Threshold Watchlist activation. Do not apply PTW-001 through PTW-005 retroactively to its completed intake, Transcript Notes, Adaptation Plan, incident selection, story architecture, approved scenes, assembled manuscript, or completed revision decisions. The Watchlist must not be used to reinterpret, reselect, reframe, or revise DA-003 development to create a match. Any later resemblance belongs only to the normal Cross-Case Pattern Observatory reconciliation after DA-003 reaches approved completed-case status and cannot be treated as evidence that the earlier adaptation was built around the Watchlist.

## Intake workflow

During every future Source Transcript Intake, consult the current Watchlist after the source has been preserved and its observable material has been recorded neutrally.

For each potentially relevant observation, record:

- Watchlist ID
- exact source observation and timestamp
- evidence layer: source fact / reported testimony / investigator interpretation / captured phenomenon / secondhand claim
- exact similarity
- important differences
- ordinary or technical alternatives
- whether the observation weakens, complicates, or merely resembles the candidate

No causal interpretation is required. Do not rewrite the intake, select incidents, change the central interpretation, or modify adaptation choices to make a candidate fit.

## Initial watchlist

### PTW-001 — Recorded speech with uneven media survival

**Status:** Active watch candidate; non-canonical.

**Current basis:** DA-001 leaves unresolved why a complete phrase survives only on one raw camera track. DA-002 contains unresolved speech-like audio and recorder modulation.

**Watch for:** Speech-like material present on one recorder but absent from another; raw-versus-processed differences; independent transcriptions; stable versus unstable wording.

**Weakening evidence:** Ordinary channel differences, compression, contamination, enhancement artifacts, expectation effects, unstable listener agreement, or repeated cases where supposedly comparable speech survives uniformly across media.

### PTW-002 — Attention, naming, replay, search, or editing

**Status:** Active watch candidate; non-canonical.

**Current basis:** DA-001 and DA-002 both leave open whether attention, labeling, replay, search, naming, or editing affects interpretation alone or what later becomes available to record.

**Watch for:** Events occurring only after playback, labeling, repeated searching, discussion, naming, or editing, while preserving sequence and contamination risks.

**Weakening evidence:** Priming, selective review, expectation, memory convergence, editorial selection, repeated non-recurrence after the same actions, or cases where the apparent sequence reverses.

### PTW-003 — Architectural uncertainty

**Status:** Active watch candidate; non-canonical.

**Current basis:** DA-001 contains a sealed service corridor and incomplete knowledge of its lock and use. DA-002 leaves architectural and search-related uncertainty relevant to interpretation.

**Watch for:** Unknown renovations, inaccessible spaces, mismatched plans, concealed circulation routes, acoustic coupling, pressure paths, undocumented doors, or mistaken spatial assumptions.

**Weakening evidence:** Later plans, inspections, renovation records, acoustic tests, airflow analysis, or participant-position reconstruction that resolves the uncertainty ordinarily.

### PTW-004 — Physical movement with incomplete coverage

**Status:** Active watch candidate; non-canonical.

**Current basis:** DA-002 contains larger fire-door movement under incomplete mechanical and participant coverage. DA-001 contains unresolved key movement and impact phenomena but is not an exact recurrence.

**Watch for:** Object or door movement where a relevant camera angle, participant location, mechanical condition, environmental variable, or preceding action is missing.

**Weakening evidence:** Mechanical reconstruction, pressure or vibration effects, participant contact, camera blind spots, ordinary object instability, or materially different movement conditions.

### PTW-005 — Ambiguous recording artifacts resistant to stable interpretation

**Status:** Active watch candidate; non-canonical.

**Current basis:** DA-001 contains unresolved audio fragments and a booth image; DA-002 contains unresolved speech-like audio and recorder modulation.

**Watch for:** Artifacts that remain ambiguous across repeated review, different devices, enhancement methods, or independent observers rather than resolving cleanly into either mundane or paranormal interpretations.

**Weakening evidence:** Codec or sensor artifacts, enhancement-induced structure, pareidolia, source contamination, repeatable technical reproduction, or stable ordinary identification.

## Completed-case reconciliation

After a case reaches approved completed status and its Continuity Report and authorized canon updates are complete, reconcile every Watchlist observation against the Cross-Case Pattern Observatory.

Classify the completed-case result as one of:

- genuine recurrence
- contradiction
- failed repetition
- no meaningful comparison

A Watchlist entry may remain active, be narrowed, split, marked dormant, or be retired. Retire weak or misleading candidates rather than preserving them indefinitely merely because they were once interesting. Preserve the historical record and the reason for retirement.

Only a separate Pattern Threshold Review under the governing protocol may consider whether anything broader warrants canon review.

## Maintenance rule

Keep the Watchlist small. Prefer a few precise, falsifiable dimensions over broad thematic similarities.

### New candidate admission gate

Add a new PTW candidate only when a precise, falsifiable observation genuinely warrants continued attention across future cases. A proposed candidate must satisfy all of the following before admission:

- **Precise observation:** State the proposed pattern narrowly enough that a later case can meaningfully match, contradict, or fail to reproduce it. Broad themes, moods, symbols, or generic paranormal similarities do not qualify.
- **Concrete basis:** Cite the exact source-derived or approved-story observation that prompted consideration, with provenance and evidence layer preserved. A candidate cannot be created solely from creative extrapolation, plot utility, or an investigator/witness theory.
- **Distinct analytical value:** Explain why the observation is not already adequately covered by an existing PTW entry, Open Mystery item, Contradiction Farming record, or Cross-Case Pattern Observatory candidate.
- **Falsifiability:** State in advance what future evidence, failed recurrence, ordinary explanation, dependency, or material difference would weaken, split, retire, or falsify the candidate.
- **Continued-attention value:** Explain what future observation could test the proposition without changing intake, adaptation, research, or story decisions to seek a match.
- **Ordinary alternatives:** Record plausible technical, environmental, psychological, investigative, editorial, cultural, coincidence, contamination, or unknown alternatives relevant to the proposed observation.

If any admission requirement is missing, do not create a PTW number. Preserve the observation in its proper case-specific record and revisit it only if later evidence independently supplies the missing justification.

Do not add a candidate solely because it would be useful for a future plot. Do not promote vocabulary, numbers, symbols, equipment responses, entity labels, or witness theories merely because they recur superficially. Every active candidate must state what future evidence could weaken or falsify it.
