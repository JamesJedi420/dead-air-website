## Overview

DA-002 is a supervised return case at Cedar Plain High School. A student documentary crew brings a medium into the building for a final cleansing. Facilities worker Diane Madsen permits the session only under written conditions governing access, consent, attribution, source custody, private identities, and the language used for uncertain events.

The session produces a failed photograph, equipment interruptions, broad impressions that acquire biographies through group contributions, a fire door that crosses roughly half an opening under incomplete coverage, a bounded ritual followed by reported relief, and unidentified audio that receives incompatible listener interpretations.
